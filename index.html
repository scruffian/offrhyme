Spoonerizer
Perfect rhymes — exact match from last stressed vowel
Compound rhymes — match on secondary stress (e.g. "lime" with "daytime")
Voicing twins — same vowel, same consonants, voicing flipped (e.g. "trees" with "geese")
Slant rhymes — same short vowel with matching or similar final consonants
Front rhymes — match on the start of the word
Near rhymes — same vowel, consonants bent around it (e.g. "trees" with "leaves")
Consonance rhymes — same consonants, different vowel (e.g. "leaves" with "loves")
Fill intensity = word commonness (perfect / compound / voicing / slant / consonance) or match quality (front / near).
